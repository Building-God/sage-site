We are committed to complying with the Australian Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

By using Sage, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

2. What Information We Collect

2.1 Information Collected Automatically

When you interact with Sage in a Discord server, we may collect and process:

  • Discord User ID -- a numeric identifier assigned to you by Discord. We do not collect your Discord username, email address, or real name unless you explicitly provide them in a message.
  • Discord Server (Guild) ID -- identifies the server in which the interaction occurs.
  • Discord Channel ID -- identifies the channel in which a message was sent.
  • Message content -- the text of messages sent to or processed by Sage. This is used to facilitate debate analysis, argument mapping, and AI-assisted conversation functions.
  • Conversation transcripts -- sequences of messages within a Sage-facilitated session, used to maintain context for AI analysis.
  • Timestamps -- the date and time of interactions.

2.2 Information You Provide Voluntarily

If you contact us directly (e.g. by email), we collect the information you choose to provide, such as your name and email address.

2.3 What We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect Discord passwords, payment information, or OAuth tokens.
  • We do not access private messages (DMs) unless Sage is specifically invited to a DM channel.
  • We do not collect data from channels where Sage has not been granted access.
  • We do not use tracking pixels, cookies, or browser fingerprinting.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected for the following purposes:

  • Providing the Service -- analysing message content to facilitate structured debate, argument mapping, perspective surfacing, and AI-assisted conversation moderation.
  • Improving the Service -- understanding how Sage is used to improve its accuracy, relevance, and features.
  • Safety and abuse prevention -- detecting misuse, policy violations, or harmful content.
  • Support -- responding to questions, data access requests, or complaints.
  • Legal compliance -- meeting obligations under applicable law.

We do not use your information to serve advertisements. We do not sell your personal information to any third party.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Under the Australian Privacy Act 1988, we collect and process personal information on the following bases:

  • Consent -- by choosing to interact with Sage in a Discord server, you consent to processing as described here.
  • Legitimate interests -- operating and improving the Service, preventing abuse, and ensuring security.
  • Legal obligation -- complying with applicable laws and responding to lawful requests.

Message content and conversation transcripts are sent to AI model providers for analysis. Current providers include:

  • OpenAI (OpenAI, LLC, USA) -- Privacy Policy. Used for natural language understanding and generation.

6. Cross-Border Disclosure

By using Sage, you acknowledge that your data may be transferred to, and processed in, countries outside Australia -- including the United States -- by our sub-processors. We take reasonable steps to ensure that overseas recipients handle your information in a manner consistent with the Australian Privacy Principles.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this policy, or as required by law.

  • Session transcripts -- retained for the duration of the Sage session plus a short operational window (typically 30 days), then deleted.
  • User IDs and interaction logs -- retained for up to 12 months for operational and safety purposes, then anonymised or deleted.
  • Support correspondence -- retained for 2 years from the date of last contact.

You may request deletion of your data at any time (see Section 9).

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss, unauthorised access, modification, and disclosure. These measures include access controls, encryption in transit, and limiting data access to personnel who need it. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Rights

Under the Australian Privacy Act 1988, you have the right to:

  • Access -- request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction -- request that inaccurate, outdated, or incomplete information be corrected.
  • Deletion -- request that your personal information be deleted (subject to legal retention obligations).
  • Complaint -- lodge a complaint if you believe we have breached the Australian Privacy Principles.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at harry@harrykey.com. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are dissatisfied with our response, you may lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

10. Children's Privacy

Sage is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at harry@harrykey.com and we will delete it promptly.

Users of Discord must comply with Discord's minimum age requirements (13 in most jurisdictions, 16 in some EU countries).

11. Discord-Specific Disclosures

Sage operates as a Discord bot and complies with Discord's Developer Policy and Developer Terms of Service. In particular:

  • Sage only accesses message content to provide the core bot functionality (debate analysis and conversation facilitation).
  • Sage does not store message content beyond what is necessary for the current session or operational logs.
  • Sage does not share data collected via Discord with third parties for advertising purposes.
  • Server administrators who add Sage to their server are responsible for ensuring their members are aware that Sage processes message content as described in this policy.
