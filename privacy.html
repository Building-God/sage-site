Privacy Policy

Sage by Spark AI · Updated 22 September 2026

Sage is operated by Spark AI Pty Ltd in Australia. This policy explains how Sage processes discussion data and how its public website and live board work.

What Sage processes

  • Voice audio from the Discord voice channel Sage joins, to produce transcripts and responses.
  • Conversation transcripts, timestamps, Discord user IDs, usernames, display names, roles, and server/channel/message identifiers.
  • Message content addressed to Sage, offered links, uploads and attachment metadata, evidence petitions and annotations, debate-map records, and operational logs. Logs can include excerpts of edited or deleted messages.
  • Stored participant profiles, including preferred names, issue-specific debate and conduct results, and summaries or tags about stated beliefs, identity, and communication patterns. AI-generated descriptions can be incorrect.
  • Information you provide when contacting the operator.

How information is used

Sage uses this information to follow discussion, generate a debate map, respond in text or voice, associate contributions with participants, and maintain context. Saved transcripts and board records also support troubleshooting, replay, and evaluation of the service.

For new-message evidence collection, Sage uses the active voice session's text chat and explicitly authorized channels and their threads. This is not a promise that all logging is limited to those channels: message edit/delete logging and member synchronization also operate.

Who can see discussion information

The live board is public when available. Visitors may see participant names, discussion text, transcript excerpts, Sage's analysis, and marks added to evidence. Someone viewing a public discussion may make their own copy. The website does not provide an archive browser.

When browser participation is enabled, Discord sign-in identifies room participants who add evidence marks or control shared video playback. Sage checks the actor's Discord ID against current room membership. Evidence marks store that ID and show the attributed mark; shared playback shows who controlled it. Opening the public board alone does not grant these controls.

The operator can access stored service records for operating and supporting Sage. Avoid sharing confidential or sensitive personal information in a public discussion. A server invitation is not a guarantee that every participant has agreed to recording or public display.

Service providers and overseas processing

Discord carries the messages and voice activity. Sage sends audio or text and relevant context to external services for transcription, AI analysis, and speech generation. Depending on the configured feature or fallback, integrations include OpenAI, Groq, Deepgram, ElevenLabs, Anthropic, Google Gemini, and Z.ai. These services may process information outside Australia. Their handling of requests is governed by the applicable service terms; this policy does not promise zero provider retention or that every provider has identical data practices.

This website is hosted using OpenAI Sites infrastructure. The live viewer connects through Tailscale to the operator's board service. Hosting and network services process connection information such as IP addresses and request information to deliver these pages. The board also requests a font from Google Fonts. These pages do not contain an email signup, account creation, or advertising analytics feature.

Storage and retention

Sage currently saves conversation transcripts and persists board, evidence, profile, and operational records. Transcripts are retained rather than automatically expiring after a set number of days. Automatic anonymization and an automatic erasure workflow are not currently implemented.

Deleting a Discord message, leaving a server, or removing Sage does not automatically erase stored copies. Removing a name or hashing an identifier would not necessarily make conversation content anonymous.

The website and public board connection use HTTPS. This policy does not claim that stored data is anonymized or encrypted at rest.

Access, correction, deletion, and complaints

To ask about your information, request access or correction, request deletion, or raise a privacy complaint, contact the.sage.system.email@gmail.com. Include enough context to identify the relevant conversation or account, but do not send passwords or identity documents in your initial request.

Requests require operator review; there is no self-service erasure button. Applicable rights and obligations, including under the Australian Privacy Act where it applies, are not limited by this policy. You can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner about privacy complaints.

Changes

Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date. This policy describes the present service; it does not claim that proposed future anonymization, email collection, or recap features already exist.
