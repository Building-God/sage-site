Privacy Policy

Sage by Spark AI · Updated 23 September 2026

Hi — I’m Sage. I’m an AI that hangs out in a Discord voice room, listens to the conversation, sometimes chimes in, and draws a live map of what’s being argued and who’s making sense. This page tells you, plainly, what I do with what I hear. It’s meant to be honest first and lawyer-proof second.

The short version. The room is livestreamed. If you’re in it and talking, you’re on the stream and in my transcripts. If you don’t want that, you can opt out (details below) and I’ll stop hearing and recording you — but you’re still in the room, so if you keep talking your voice can still be heard live. If you want your existing records gone, email us.

Who runs Sage. Spark AI Pty Ltd, Australia. Contact the.sage.system.email@gmail.com.

What I take in

  • Voice from the Discord voice channel I join. It becomes text.
  • Transcripts — the text of what people said, with your Discord display name, Discord user IDs, timestamps, and channel/message IDs.
  • Text chat — message content, links, and files posted in the same voice room’s chat and any other channels you’ve explicitly authorised.
  • The live debate map — claims, challenges, and counter-arguments I pulled out of the conversation, attributed to who said them.
  • Participant notes — a stored profile per participant with preferred name, past debate/conduct results in this server, and short summaries about stated positions and communication style. These are my interpretation; I can be wrong.
  • Operational logs to run and debug the bot. These can include excerpts of edited or deleted messages.
  • Contact details if you email us.

How I use it

To follow the conversation, generate the live map, respond in voice or text, remember context across turns, and — with the operator — improve and troubleshoot the service. I do not train AI models on your Discord content.

Who can see what

  • The live map is public while a session is live. Visitors see discussion text, participant names, transcript excerpts, my analysis, and evidence marks. Anyone watching can screenshot or copy anything they see. There is no browsable archive of past sessions on the website.
  • The room is livestreamed. Clips from the stream may be published. This is disclosed up front, so there’s no separate clip consent — if you don’t want to be on the stream, opt out and stop talking, or don’t join.
  • Anyone who signs in with Discord on the public board (and is currently in the voice room) can add evidence marks and control shared video playback. Those actions are attributed and visible to the public viewer.
  • The operator can access stored records to run and support Sage.

Please don’t share confidential or sensitive personal information in a public discussion. Being invited to a server is not automatic agreement from every participant to be recorded or publicly displayed — that’s on the server operator to make clear (and me, with the notice below).

How I tell people, and what “opt out” means

The first time I meet you in the room, I say hi in the room’s side chat. I tell you who I am, that the room is livestreamed, how to opt out, and where to email for data removal. I do this once, per person. No DM. No button. No “click to agree” — I run on legitimate interest under Australian and (where it applies) European privacy law, with clear notice and an easy way to object.

Opting out. Tell me out loud or in the room’s text chat. I’ll ask you to confirm; on a clear yes I stop hearing and recording you — I won’t transcribe or map you from that point. I won’t mute you (you can still talk and listen), so if you keep talking your voice is still on the livestream — the safe move is to stop talking or leave the room. Say “I consent” in text later to come back. I remember your choice.

Where your words go

Discord carries the messages and audio. To do transcription, AI analysis, and speech, I send audio or text (with the context the model needs) to external providers. Which providers depends on the feature or fallback in use; today those can include OpenAI, Groq, Deepgram, ElevenLabs, Anthropic, Google (Gemini), and Z.ai. These providers process data outside Australia and each has its own terms. This policy doesn’t promise that every provider retains nothing or that they all handle data identically.

This website is hosted on OpenAI Sites infrastructure. The live viewer reaches the operator’s board over Tailscale. Hosting and network providers process connection information (IPs, request info) to serve these pages. The board also loads a font from Google Fonts. There’s no email signup, account creation, or advertising analytics on these pages.

How long I keep it

Honest answer: for now, indefinitely. There is no automatic deletion clock. I keep transcripts, board records, evidence, profiles, and operational logs on the operator’s machine until you (or the operator) delete them. Deleting a Discord message, leaving the server, or removing me from the server does not automatically erase what I’ve already stored. A future version of this policy may add an automated retention window — this one doesn’t claim there is one.

Encryption at rest: no. Stored data lives on the operator’s Windows PC as ordinary files and a local database. HTTPS protects it in transit to and from the site and the live board, but “encrypted at rest” would be false to claim right now.

Access, correction, deletion

Email the.sage.system.email@gmail.com with your Discord name (and enough context to find the right conversation) and we’ll delete your records. Reviewed by a human, not a self-service button. Please don’t send passwords or ID documents in your first email.

Nothing on this page limits rights you have under the Australian Privacy Act (where it applies to us) or your local privacy law. You can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Changes

Updates get posted here with a revised date. This policy describes Sage as she works today. It doesn’t claim future features (automated deletion, encryption at rest, an anonymiser, a recap emailer, etc.) already exist.
