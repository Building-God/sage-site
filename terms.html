What Sage is

Sage joins a Discord voice room, listens, sometimes speaks, and draws a live map of the arguments. Everything she says or maps is her interpretation — it can be wrong. There is no guarantee the service is available; the viewer will show an offline state when nothing is live.

Using Sage

  • Play nicely. Follow Discord’s terms and the server’s rules.
  • Don’t use Sage to harass anyone, share illegal content, disrupt the service, or poke at things you shouldn’t.
  • You must meet Discord’s age and eligibility rules for your location.
  • If you run a server, make sure your participants know Sage is there, that the room is livestreamed, and get any local permissions your users need. Installing the bot is not the same as everyone in the room saying yes to being recorded.
